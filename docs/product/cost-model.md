## Spending Rule

Prototype spend should buy learning. Production spend should wait for evidence: spring TimeBite release learning, June partial-demo quality, July/August preview demand signal, working interactions, stable dimensions, vendor feedback, and BOM confidence.

September presentation needs can justify polish, packaging, photography, and controlled sample preparation. They do not by themselves justify mass inventory, custom PCB NRE, or broad collection expansion.
