# Initial Tagimata Looks

## Capsule Constraint

Do not build the entire collection first. Build enough to communicate the brand and support the integrated ecosystem demo.

## Recommended Hero Looks

### Look 01 - Day/Focus Set

- U-neck or contemporary Filipiniana-influenced top
- skinny flare pant or sharp skirt
- light blue/dark blue/white palette
- 16-point star motif placement
- flagship purse
- flagship charm

Purpose: wearable, recognizable, easiest to connect to daily productivity.

### Look 02 - Evening/Launch Look

- black halter, backless top, LBD, or longer dress concept
- white/dark accent or star motif
- optional secondary purse
- charm as jewelry/accessory attachment

Purpose: editorial and launch-ready.

### Look 03 - Alternate Filipiniana Silhouette

- medium or longer skirt/dress silhouette
- modern Filipino design influence
- less tech-forward, more brand-world-building

Purpose: shows Tagimata is a fashion language, not only merch for the app.

## Development Stages

Concept selection -> sketches/specification -> pattern -> material sourcing -> first sample -> fitting -> revision -> second sample -> photography/editorial sample -> manufacturing specification -> production decision.

## February 2027 Target

Ready for manufacturer/sourcing conversations:

- selected 2-3 hero looks
- drawings or sketches
- rough tech specs
- initial patterns or block direction
- material direction and backup materials
- sample/mockup evidence where possible
